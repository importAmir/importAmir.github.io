My name is Amirhosein Javadi, and I am a Ph.D. student in ECE at UCSD, advised by Professor [Tara Javidi](https://tjavidi.eng.ucsd.edu). Prior to joining UCSD, I received a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology in 2023. My research focuses on generative AI and multimodal learning, with an emphasis on world models, novel view synthesis, and video generation, as well as making foundation models more reliable and interpretable.

## **Experience**

- **Machine Learning Research Intern at Apple** (Jun. 2026 – Sep. 2026)
  - **Reliable Audio-Language Models.** Developed a framework that predicts, before generation, whether an Audio LLM can reliably understand a spoken input. A lightweight predictor over frozen audio-encoder representations outperforms speech-quality, uncertainty, and transcript-based baselines, and generalizes across unseen acoustic conditions and Audio LLM families.

- **Research Intern at Soter Technology, Inc.** (Jun. 2025 – Sep. 2025)
  - **Visual World Models.** Developed camera-controlled novel-view video generation from single- or multi-view images and videos, where users specify the camera trajectory, improving performance on downstream monitoring and safety tasks in complex industrial environments.
